# Eliswa

> Eliswa is a bespoke Kerala Kasavu and Tissue saree brand. We create handloom sarees rooted in Kerala's textile heritage, with a focus on authentic gold zari work, premium fabric, and made-to-order craftsmanship for festive and everyday wear.

Eliswa specializes in traditional Kerala Kasavu sarees (off-white cotton with gold zari borders) and Tissue sarees, sourced from and inspired by Kerala's handloom weaving traditions including Balaramapuram, Kuthampully, and Chendamangalam. Our current focus collection is Onam sarees, timed to Kerala's harvest festival, alongside our core bespoke Kasavu and Tissue offerings.

## Collections

- [Onam Sarees](https://eliswa.com/onam-sarees): Handwoven Kerala Kasavu sarees for Thiruvonam, Onasadya, and Onam celebrations.
- [Kasavu Sarees](https://eliswa.com/kasavu-sarees): Traditional off-white cotton sarees with gold zari borders, made to order.
- [Tissue Sarees](https://eliswa.com/tissue-sarees): Fine tissue-yarn sarees with a festive sheen, suited to evening and formal occasions.
